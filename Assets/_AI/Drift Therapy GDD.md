# Drift Therapy – GDD

## 1. Overview
Portrait, semi top-down endless drifting game. Player drives forward automatically, drifts through curves, dodges traffic, and builds combos for high scores. Focus on smooth flow, simple controls, and replayability.

Platform: Android / iOS  
Genre: Casual, Arcade, Endless Driving  
Style: Stylized, vibrant, lowpoly

---

## 2. Core Loop
Drive → Drift → Dodge → Build Combo → Increase Difficulty → Crash → Restart

Session Length: 1–3 mins

---

## 3. Controls

### Default (Touch)
- Slide finger left/right → Steering (analog)
- Tap / Hold → Drift

Notes:
- No acceleration/brake
- Drift is primary mechanic

---

## 4. Core Mechanics

### Movement
- Constant forward motion
- Player controls direction + drift only

### Drift
- Tap = initiate drift
- Hold = extend drift
- Release = straighten
- Drift increases score multiplier
- Over-drift increases risk

### Traffic
- AI vehicles (cars, trucks)
- Increasing density over time
- Slight movement variation (not static lanes)

### Collision
- Any collision = run ends
- Optional revive (ad or currency)

---

## 5. Scoring

- Distance (base score)
- Drift multiplier (combo)
- Near miss bonus

Rules:
- Continuous drift builds combo
- Breaking drift resets combo

---

## 6. Difficulty Scaling

Over time:
- Speed increases
- Traffic density increases
- Road curvature increases

## 11. USP

- One-touch drift + analog steering
- Drift = movement + scoring
- Flow-based (non-lane) driving
- High replayability via combo + near-miss system

---

## 12. Design Goals

- Easy to learn, hard to master
- Smooth, responsive controls
- Short, addictive sessions
- Minimal UI, maximum feel
